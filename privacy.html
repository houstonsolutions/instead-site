Privacy Policy — Instead

Last updated: 28 July 2026 Published by MadApps Studios, LLC

Instead is a tool for getting through a craving. What you put into it is personal, so the short version is this: your data stays on your phone.

What Instead stores on your device

All of it lives in the app's own storage on your phone. None of it is uploaded, synced, or visible to us:

  • Your sobriety start date, streak, and lifetime clean days
  • The habit you named and what it used to cost you
  • Cravings you've beaten, XP, and your best game score
  • Journal entries you write
  • The contact you save as "your person" (name and phone number)
  • Your settings

We cannot read any of this. There is no account, no login, and no server copy. If you uninstall the app, it is gone — including from us, because we never had it. Use Milestones → Back up your data to save a copy you control.

What Instead never does

  • No advertising, and no ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal information
  • No location data
  • No contacts access — "your person" is typed in by you
  • No account, email, or phone number required

The one thing that leaves your phone, only if you send it

The app has a beta feedback button (a small ladybug). It is off by default. You turn it on in Milestones, and it does nothing unless you tap it.

When you tap it and press Send, we receive:

  • The message you typed
  • A screenshot of the screen you were on, taken at the moment you tapped
  • A random, anonymous install ID — not linked to your name, email, or device
  • The time you sent it

Nothing else is included. Your journal, streak, and saved contact are not sent. The screenshot is only ever taken when you tap the button, never in the background — but note that if the screen you were on showed a journal entry, that entry will be visible in the screenshot you send.

This data goes to our own server (Supabase, hosted in the United States), is used solely to find and fix bugs, and is not shared with anyone else.

Crisis resources

Instead can dial 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) and a contact you save. Dialling happens in your phone's own dialer. We do not place calls, record calls, or receive any information about calls you make.

Instead is not medical care, therapy, or a crisis service. It does not replace professional treatment or emergency help. If you are in danger, call 988 or your local emergency number.

Children

Instead is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Keep a copy: Milestones → Back up your data
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app (see Delete your data)
  • Stop sending feedback: turn the beta feedback button off in Milestones
  • Remove feedback you already sent: email us with the date you sent it and
we will delete it

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app.
