Why ninety seconds

A craving feels permanent. It isn't. Urges tend to rise, crest, and fade — often in just a few minutes.

You don't have to beat it forever. You just have to get through the next ninety seconds. Then the next one, if it comes. Instead™ is built for that one small stretch of time — the part that feels impossible right before it passes.
